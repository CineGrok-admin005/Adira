How to use this tool with Claude

What this tool does

This is a free, local carousel builder. You paste slide content, pick a theme, customise each slide, and download 1080×1080px PNGs ready to upload to Instagram, LinkedIn, or any platform. No account needed. No data leaves your device.

The exact format to ask Claude for

Ask Claude to write your carousel and tell it to use this format exactly:

Give me a carousel in this exact format: SLIDE 1: [cover headline] | [one line of subtext] SLIDE 2: [LABEL IN CAPS] | [Heading text] | [Body text 1-2 sentences] SLIDE 3: [LABEL IN CAPS] | [Heading text] | [Body text] ...continue for each slide... SLIDE [N]: Find your people. | [your website or CTA] Rules: - Use | to separate label, heading, body - Slide 1 is always the cover (headline + subtext only) - Last slide is always the CTA - Keep headings under 8 words - Keep body text under 30 words - Also write an Instagram caption below the slides

Example prompt for a craft breakdown carousel

I'm making an Instagram carousel for [YOUR BRAND]. Audience: [describe your audience]. Topic: [your topic] Pillar: Craft Breakdown (step-by-step technique) Give me 8 slides in this format: SLIDE 1: [hook headline] | [one subtext line] SLIDE 2: [LABEL] | [Heading] | [Body] SLIDE 3: [LABEL] | [Heading] | [Body] SLIDE 4: [LABEL] | [Heading] | [Body] SLIDE 5: [LABEL] | [Heading] | [Body] SLIDE 6: [LABEL] | [Heading] | [Body] SLIDE 7: [LABEL] | [Heading] | [Body] SLIDE 8: Find your people. | [website] Then write an Instagram caption: - Line 1: main keyword + sharpest version of the point - 2-3 sentences of value - One question to drive comments - Hashtags (5 max, specific)

Content pillars you can ask Claude for

Craft Breakdown Industry Pulse Filmmaker Spotlight Honest Process Educational Framework Tools & Resources Community Question

Tell Claude which pillar you want. Each has a different structure. Claude will write the slides to fit that structure.

Pro tips for better slides

  • Headings with line breaks: Use \n in the heading field to break across lines (e.g. "First line\nSecond line")
  • Background images: Upload a photo per slide. The theme text renders on top. Good for location-based content.
  • Text alignment: Switch between Left, Centre, Right per slide in the editor.
  • Custom accent colour: Pick any hex colour to match your brand identity.
  • Download order: Use "Download as ZIP" — files are named 01.png, 02.png etc. Upload in that order.
  • Font size: Adjust per slide if a heading is too long or too short for the space.
  • Variable count: Making a Reel script card? Set count to 1. Making a long tutorial? Set to 15.
